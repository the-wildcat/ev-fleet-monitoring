How these numbers are calculated
  • Energy used: drops in battery charge between readings while driving × battery capacity.
  • Energy cost (used for cost per km): energy used ÷ charging efficiency ({{ report.settings.charging_efficiency_pct|int }}%) × tariff (currently ₹{{ "%g"|format(report.settings.energy_tariff_inr_per_kwh) }}/kWh; each day keeps the tariff that applied then). Basing cost on consumption keeps cost per km stable no matter when vehicles charge.
  • Charging: charge added while plugged in ÷ charging efficiency = electricity bought, and what was spent on it (matches the bill).
  • Maintenance: costs entered on service records in this period.
  • Petrol comparison: same distance at {{ "%g"|format(r.settings.ice_km_per_l) }} km/L and ₹{{ "%g"|format(r.settings.petrol_price_inr_per_l) }}/L. CO₂ compares {{ config.PETROL_EMISSION_KG_PER_L }} kg/L for petrol with the grid electricity behind the energy used, at India's average of {{ config.GRID_EMISSION_KG_PER_KWH }} kg/kWh (CEA).
  • Figures are rolled up into daily totals every hour (today's refresh every few minutes), so they persist after raw telemetry is pruned. Admins can change tariff and comparison values in Settings.
